What the words mean

Bank
Damage you decide to keep. It becomes your share of the chest.
The chest
The pot. It grows every night nobody wins it.
Crack
When a swing bursts the chest open and it pays out.
Finder's cut
20% extra to whoever's swing cracked it. The rest splits by share.
$RONKESTR
A Ronin token. Part of the chest is paid in it, not in dollars.
$BURN · rORE
Ronin tokens you spend to craft a weapon. That spend funds the pot.
