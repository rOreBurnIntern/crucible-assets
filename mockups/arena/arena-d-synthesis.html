How to play

  1. Feed the Warden optional, do it first

    Send $RONKESTR before you attack. Every attack after it hits 15% harder, up to three offerings a day. It does nothing to attacks you have already made.

  2. Forge a weapon

    About $0.03, paid in $BURN, rORE and $RONKESTR. Rarer weapons roll bigger dice, so they hit harder.

  3. Attack the Warden

    Your first five attacks are always safe. Each one adds damage to the weapon.

  4. Bank it, or keep attacking

    After the fifth, every attack can shatter the weapon and cost you all the damage it is holding. Banking makes that damage permanent and ends the run.

  5. Banked damage is your share

    When anyone cracks the chest it splits by banked damage. Crack it yourself and take 20% off the top first. Any attack can be the one, about 1 in 3,000.
