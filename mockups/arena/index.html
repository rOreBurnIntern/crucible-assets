Crucible Arena · three directions

arena.

Three ways to rebuild the screen you actually play on. Same content in all three, same real data from game day 29. Tap each one, then swing at the Warden.

Mockups only. Nothing here is wired to the game and no live code was touched. Each one carries five things and nothing else: the chest, the Warden, your weapon and controls, the offering, and a thin ticker. Everything else you have today (hunt log, chest log, boards, earnings) moves to a separate Records page. Use the small state control to switch between having a weapon and not.

Direction A
The Purse
The prize is the hero. The pot is the biggest thing on screen and your share is stamped underneath it like a ticket. The controls live in a rail pinned to the bottom that never scrolls away.
Signature: the pot counts up on a fill line marked with the last crack.
Direction B
The Fight Bill
The boss is the hero. Tonight's Warden fills the page and the type sits on the art like a boxing bill. The purse hangs off the name. You act directly on the boss.
Signature: crack the chest and the bill tears in half. Try the crack control.
Direction C
The Foundry Line
The loop is the hero. Forge, strike, bank, chest, running left to right. Where you stand is lit and the rest is dim, so the order of play is a fact on screen instead of an instruction.
Signature: banking pours damage down a molten channel into the chest.

For comparison, the screen these replace: desktop today and mobile today, which runs 4,579px on a phone.
